# Alpha Platform Governance

**Status:** Proposed for immediate implementation upon management approval  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-06  
**Audience:** Managing Director Ravinder Birdi, CIO Sam, IT Operations Chief Nadine, GIPS Application Support, IT Infrastructure, IT Security, Alpha business stakeholders.

This directory establishes the proposed **single operational ownership model** for both the existing Alpha/OpenVMS environment and the GDPS Windows replacement program.

## Controlled drafts
1. [Executive Ownership Brief](01_Executive_Ownership_Brief.md) — concise leadership decision, governance diagram and immediate actions.
2. [Detailed Operating Model](02_Detailed_Operating_Model.md) — scope, workflow, security, purchasing, COOP, change control, and escalation.
3. [Contacts and Responsibility Matrix](03_Contacts_and_Responsibility_Matrix.md) — functional roles and blank individual-name/contact placeholders.

## Governing principle
GIPS Application Support is the **single operational coordinator/accountable application-platform owner**. IT Infrastructure and IT Security retain technical execution and their independent mandatory approval authorities. Business units retain ownership of business requirements, rules, outcomes, and workflow definitions, but do not independently commission Alpha platform hardware or technical changes.

These documents are **proposed governance**, not evidence that a transfer has already been formally approved or executed. The proposed effective date is **immediately upon CIO/management authorization**, with no phased transfer.
